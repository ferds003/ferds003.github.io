Country Finalist of Schneider Go Green (2022) 🌱⚡

Created on March 28, 2022

2022

🔥 Schneider Go Green 2022 is a global student competition that challenges young innovators to design disruptive ideas tackling critical social and environmental issues — including rising energy consumption, sustainability, and urban challenges.

I’m proud to have been a Country Finalist, receiving seed funding to kickstart our startup idea, and went on to represent the Philippines at the competition’s regional stage! 🇵🇭🌏

Again this is a continuous endeavor from me to kickstart the idea of a smart compact food waste digester for homeowners and restaurants!!

🌿 Our Innovation: Compact Food Waste Digester

We developed a personalized anaerobic digester system optimized for homeowners and restaurants, targeting sustainable energy production and food waste management.

🧠 My Role and Contributions:

  • Designed the methodology for proof-of-concept and presented technical findings to the judging panel 📊
  • Outlined detailed product specifications to make the digester functional and scalable for everyday users 🔧
  • Created a 3D animation to visualize material flow and simulate preliminary user interactions with the system 🎥✨

This opportunity further deepened my passion for sustainable engineering and gave me a platform to scale technical ideas into real-world solutions. Big thanks to Schneider Electric for the platform and mentorship! 🙌
