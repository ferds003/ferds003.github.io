### OVERVIEW:
The motivation for this project was that during the COVID lockdown, many have been doing home gardening as a form of personal activity. We want to provide <u>"plantitos" and "plantitas"</u> customized gradening plants for their various project needs. 

This business simulation involved creating and managing a mock manufacturing company specializing in customized gardening pots for landscaping projects, interior decorators, and gardening enthusiasts. The simulation covered the full operational lifecycle, including:

**Business Planning** – Defined mission, vision, and strategic goals focusing on sustainability, customization, and nationwide expansion.

**Organizational Structure** – Designed a vertical functional departmental structure to ensure specialized focus on R&D, manufacturing, sales, and logistics.

**Product & Process Design** – Developed standard and customized pot specifications with a factory layout optimized for efficient production and storage.

**Operations & Inventory Management** – Implemented a periodic inventory control system with ABC classification (Grade A: Ceramic & Clay, Grade B: Polycarbonate) and integrated IT-based real-time inventory tracking.

**Financial Analysis** – Calculated break-even point (6,156 units), prepared balance sheet and income statement, and proposed reinvestment strategies for growth.

## LEARNINGS and OPINIONS:
As synthesis, I was able to learn Operations Efficiency; Demand & Capacity Alignment; Inventory Risk Management; Financial Decision-Making; 

This was an introduction to operations management that We did as a freshman. This was further supplemented with the learnings on engineering economics, technopreneuership, and engineering production management. I believe that I'm able to apply this when I have a small business to attend to that does production in a small scale. Further down the line, I'm hoping that this can be applied in a real-world situation but for now, I have demonstrated here my capabilities to conduct simple business management as well as make this as a service for others when I presented a simple SWOT analysis of a partner organization for this course.
