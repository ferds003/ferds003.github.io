## Dimensional Measurements

__SUMMARY__: A key takeaway that stuck with me for this topic is the parallax error which is a common error in measurements. This error is caused by the misalignment of the eye and the instrument, which can lead to inaccurate readings. To minimize this error, it is important to keep the eye parallel to the instrument and to use a straight edge to align the instrument with the object. ## Gauges

__PERSONAL SUMMARY__: In memory, I saw once gauge blocks and how they were applied in oil for a wringing process in the metrology lab in DOST-MIRDC. It never ceases to amaze me how these form the very definition of precision lengths that is being applied worldwide and in local industry as the definition of some of our fundamental measurements.
