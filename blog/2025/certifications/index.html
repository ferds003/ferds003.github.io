OVERVIEW: One of the many things that I want to establish in my life as a constant is being a lifelong learner. To not be defined by the grades or numbers in a sheet but on the learnings and projects that have made significant contributions to others. To achieve this, I look outward in getting upskillings and experiences that is not taught formally but is needed in recent advancements in our field. This proves my learnings to others and how I can be of help by applying these transformative needs. Below is a growing set of my certifications!

DATACAMP:

Evers ince I got student access to datacamp where I both got free access to the courses for 3-months via GDSC-DLSU chapter (huge thanks!) and another 3-months of access via Github Student Developer Pack, I have been grinding to absorb data science concepts and tools that was never really pointed-out in my minor degree classes. Since this industry is ever-growing some teaching in college may be outdated for the real-world. Datacamp bridges this gap and offers a wide quality tutorials that is exercise-first approach and is packed in smaller bits which I like. So whenever I have a time, I finish one course. Below is the courses that I took as well as completion of tracks that enables me to be job-ready. It focuses on the associate track for data scientst and analyst which was my learning direction as of writing.

My Datacamp certificate of completion for the courses I took in 2024.

Now, below is another set of certification of accomplishment in my recent grind to learn more about SQL in the wild as well as proper development under the python language. I was formally introduced to the whole lifecyle where I had assessments covering from: unit-testings, MLOps, and object-oriented programming!

UDEMY:

Udemy is my go-to online platform to get learnings from others with a known certificate of accomplishment once I have finished a course. Of course there are many alternatives that are free with similar quality content but Udemy is a platform that upholds that quality via customer ratings and feeback so I dont have to waste time knowing if the teachings that is presented in the course is right for me or not. Plus side is that you get to keep the course forever and has a 30-day refund period if I dont like it. Thankfully, the learnings have been eye-openers on what I need to do to level-up my career! For me, that would be on embedded systems and the known robotic framework, ROS. Below is my certificate of these concepts.

My udemy course certificate of completion focused on robotics and embedded systems
