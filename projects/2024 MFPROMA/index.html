Optimizing Production in a Dairy Cooperative

My service learning project for GenTri's Best Dairy Products 🐄

OVERVIEW:

The General Trias Dairy Raisers Multipurpose Cooperative (GTDRMC), known for its "GenTri's Best" dairy products, faces challenges in yogurt production, including limited shelf life, rapid urbanization impacting milk quality, and capacity constraints during peak seasons. A study was conducted to optimize the flavored yogurt production through production management methods. The study used qualitative forecasting (sales force composite) and capacity planning analysis to address these issues. Market research indicated a potential to target the younger generation with Greek yogurt and frozen yogurt variants.

The sales force composite method was used to forecast demand, considering seasonality and market conditions. Capacity planning analysis revealed a throughput time of 885 minutes for yogurt production, with a design capacity of 100 pieces per day. The fermentation process was identified as a bottleneck. The study recommends balancing supply and demand through forecasting, addressing technology constraints, and building for change to accommodate increased capacity.

FINDINGS:

The paper’s scope is limited to GTDRMC’s flavored yogurt production, focusing on non-inventory optimization techniques. It uses qualitative methods and is limited to two productivity forecasting and capacity planning methods, excluding layout and job design analysis. The methodology involves forecasting using sales force composite and capacity planning to optimize resource movement. The analysis includes market research and assumptions to model a forecast and improve capacity measurements. The paper concludes by recommending the use of qualitative forecasting and capacity planning methods to optimize yogurt production, suggesting further exploration of other methods and automation for increased productivity.

LEARNINGS:

With the use of production management techniquies and tools, we were able to provide the cooperative qualitative forecasting (sales force composite), capacity planning analysis and gantt chart detailing the time study for the job.

We highlight in our report the following:

  • Market research suggests targeting younger consumers with Greek and frozen yogurt.
  • Sales force composite forecasting helps manage supply and demand fluctuations, considering seasonality.
  • Capacity planning identifies bottlenecks (fermentation process) and inefficiencies in yogurt production.
  • Throughput time for yogurt production is 885 minutes, with a design capacity of 100 pieces/day.
  • Effective capacity is reduced due to planned and unplanned resource unavailability.
  • Technology upgrades and facility expansion are recommended to increase capacity and reduce bottlenecks.
