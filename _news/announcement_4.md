We put DLSU in the map with this win in this competition. 🌍 I'm proud to share that I participated in the **Fowler Global Social Innovation Challenge (GSIC)**, a platform that empowers students from partner universities around the world to engage with the **UN’s Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** by developing social innovations that address pressing **environmental and social challenges** — locally and globally.

Again this a continuation of our iteration for the team's idea of a compact anaerobic digesters with the idea to sell this and be of use to food concessionaires and kitchen cafeterias.~
