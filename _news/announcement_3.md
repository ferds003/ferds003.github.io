Sharing the pub made for me at this event thru this [link](https://www.facebook.com/DLSMUNCOM/posts/pfbid02nFcVuoxCdBK2tFSykfut2fBzYPyEunnCuLasBDK5sCe1YnxQJ3J6LYxxForwYKhl) here! <br>

🌟 **Excited to share that I was named one of the winners** of *Echoes of Ecology: A Scientific Monologue*, held last **Saturday, November 20, 2021**!

This unique competition challenged participants to craft and deliver powerful scientific monologues that highlight innovative **solutions for building climate-ready communities** — blending science, creativity, and advocacy in one inspiring stage. 🎙️🌱

## 💬 My Monologue & Message

I took this opportunity to communicate the **urgency of climate adaptation** and empower audiences through a narrative built on **scientific understanding**, **community resilience**, and **ecological responsibility**. As a young scientist and advocate, I believe in the power of communication to spark action and stewardship for our shared environment 🌏🧪

## 🏆 Why This Win Matters

- 💡 Recognized for both **scientific depth** and **storytelling impact**
- 🌿 Emphasized **sustainability**, **climate preparedness**, and **youth engagement**


Thanks DLSUMUNCOM organizers, especially Lanz Chua and Rom Miller (got introduced to this kind power couple at this event!) for arranging this and fellow participants and judges for an inspiring event ! 🌍✨
